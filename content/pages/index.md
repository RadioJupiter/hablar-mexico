# Hablar México

A course in spoken Mexican Spanish, built for real conversation: the things you say at a taquería, in a market, on the street, and in someone's home. It is written for an adult who already reads English. You study by listening, repeating, and speaking, not by translating made-up sentences.

This first stretch is the beginner start: nine units. Work through them in order. Each one is a situation you will actually meet in Mexico.

## How to use a unit

1. Read **Learn**. It tells you what the situation requires and why a form is chosen. The Listen button on that block reads the explanation in English.
2. Play the **model conversation** with Listen. That button speaks the Spanish, in a Mexican voice when your browser has one.
3. Go through the **chunk list** with the Hear buttons. Learn whole phrases, not loose words.
4. Read the **pronunciation** note, then **shadow**: play the conversation again and speak along.
5. Do the **role-play**. Your lines are blank. Say them out loud. Open a hint only if you need it.
6. Write the **Check** answers, then use the answer key. Finish with the one-page review sheet.

There are no points, streaks, or rewards. If a check item is wrong, say the line again from memory the next day.

The method, and what this course will not do, are on the [How it works](method.html) page. The dictionaries behind Mexican usage are on [Sources](sources.html).
