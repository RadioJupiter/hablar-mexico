Units

Beginner start, in order. Do not skip ahead to a later situation until you can say the earlier lines out loud without reading them.

Unit 1 · Greetings and small talkOpening a conversation, tú and usted, and asking someone to repeat. Unit 2 · At a taquería or caféOrdering, asking for the bill, and a few food words you will actually use. Unit 3 · The market and pricesNumbers, pesos, and asking what the kilo costs. Unit 4 · Directions and transportWhere something is, how to get there, and the word camión. Unit 5 · Meeting peopleIntroducing yourself and talking about family, without oversharing. Unit 6 · Invitations and being a guestSetting a time, coming in, and sitting down to a meal. Unit 7 · The corner store and the pharmacyAsking whether they have it, paying by card or cash, and pharmacy questions. Unit 8 · Likes, plans, and the weatherMe gusta and me gustan, voy a, and easy weather small talk. Unit 9 · When something goes wrongNot understanding, asking for help, lost things, and feeling unwell.

Each unit links to its answer key and to a one-page review sheet.
