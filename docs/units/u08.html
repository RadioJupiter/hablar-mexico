Unit 8 · Likes, plans, and the weather

Once the greetings are done, conversation needs something to run on. This unit gives you three easy topics: what you like, what you are going to do, and the weather. Together they carry a lot of small talk.

Likes, plans, and the weather

Likes, plans, and the weather

About 45 minutes · Situation: keeping a friendly conversation going

Learn

What you like: gustar. Spanish does not say “I like coffee” the English way. It says, roughly, “coffee pleases me”: Me gusta el café. The thing you like is the subject, so the verb matches it. One thing or an activity takes gusta: Me gusta el café, Me gusta caminar. Several things take gustan: Me gustan los tacos. The small word in front shows who likes it: me (me), te (you, tú), le (you, usted, or him or her). ¿Te gusta la comida mexicana? ¿Le gusta el café? To say no, put no first: No me gusta el frío. To like something a lot: Me encanta. You met me encantaría in Unit 6; me encanta is its present form, “I love it.”

Asking back. Short follow-ups keep the conversation moving. ¿Y a ti? is “And you?” after a gustar line (tú); ¿Y a usted? is the formal version. A mí también is “Me too” when you agree with a liking; A mí no is “Not me.” Use a mí, not yo, in these replies, because the verb is still gustar.

Plans: voy a + verb. The easiest future is ir a + an infinitive, just like English “going to.” Voy a descansar (I'm going to rest). ¿Qué vas a hacer el fin de semana? (What are you going to do this weekend? tú). With usted: ¿Qué va a hacer el fin de semana? Answer with voy a + one or two verbs: Voy a ir al mercado y voy a visitar a mi familia. Notice a before a person you visit: visitar a mi familia.

The weather. Most weather lines use hace or está. Hace calor (it's hot), hace frío (it's cold), hace sol (it's sunny), hace viento (it's windy). Está nublado (it's cloudy). Rain uses the verb llover: Está lloviendo (it's raining now), Va a llover (it's going to rain). A common opener is ¡Qué calor! (it's so hot!), and you can invite agreement with ¿verdad?: Qué calor, ¿verdad?

Model conversation

Two neighbors talk at a bus stop on a hot afternoon. The plans are invented.

Rosa: ¡Qué calor!, ¿verdad? So hot, isn't it?

Neighbor: Sí, hace mucho calor. Pero dicen que va a llover. Yes, it's very hot. But they say it's going to rain.

Rosa: Ojalá. ¿Qué vas a hacer el fin de semana? I hope so. What are you going to do this weekend?

Neighbor: Voy a ir al mercado y voy a cocinar. Me encanta cocinar. I'm going to go to the market and I'm going to cook. I love cooking.

Rosa: ¡Qué bien! A mí también me gusta. ¿Te gusta la comida picante? How nice! I like it too. Do you like spicy food?

Neighbor: Un poco. Me gustan los tacos con salsa verde. ¿Y a ti? A little. I like tacos with green salsa. And you?

Rosa: A mí me encanta la salsa roja. Bueno, ahí viene mi camión. I love red salsa. Well, here comes my bus.

Neighbor: Nos vemos. Que te vaya bien. See you. Take care.

Pronunciation

Gusta is GOOS-tah; gustan adds a final n, GOOS-tahn, and that n carries the meaning, so say it clearly. Encanta is en-KAHN-tah. Lloviendo is yoh-VYEN-doh, with the Mexican y sound for ll. Nublado is noo-BLAH-doh. Ojalá is oh-hah-LAH: the j is a breathy h, and the stress is on the last syllable. Verdad ends softly; the final d is light, almost a th in “this.”

Remember

Me gusta + one thing or an activity, me gustan + several things. Ask back with ¿y a ti? and answer a mí también or a mí no. Plans: voy a + verb. Weather: hace calor / frío / sol, está nublado, está lloviendo, va a llover.

Watch me · shadowing

Play the model and speak the neighbor's lines. Listen for gusta against gustan in the middle of the conversation, and copy the rise on ¿verdad? and ¿y a ti? Then play it again and speak Rosa's lines, so you practice both asking and answering.

Together · you speak

Someone you know chats with you on a hot day. Say your lines out loud.

They say: ¡Qué calor!, ¿verdad?

You:

They ask: ¿Qué vas a hacer el fin de semana?

You:

They say: Me encantan los tamales. ¿Y a ti?

You:

Hint 1: the weather

Sí, hace mucho calor. You can add Dicen que va a llover.

Hint 2: your plans

Voy a descansar y voy a ir al mercado. Use voy a before each verb, or once: Voy a descansar e ir al mercado.

Hint 3: likes

A mí también. Or A mí no, pero me gustan los tacos.

Check

These questions use only what this unit taught. Write the answers, then look at the key.

  1. Why is it me gusta el café but me gustan los tacos?
  2. Ask a friend (tú) whether they like spicy food. Then ask the same of someone you address as usted.
  3. Someone says Me gusta caminar. You like it too. What do you say?
  4. Say that you are going to rest this weekend.
  5. Say “It's cold,” “It's cloudy,” and “It's going to rain.”
  6. What does Ojalá mean in reply to Va a llover?

Practice

  1. Write three sentences about what you like and one about what you do not like, using both gusta and gustan.
  2. Write two lines of small talk about the weather and one line about your weekend plans.
