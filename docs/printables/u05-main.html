Unit 5 · Meeting people · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the unit's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Te presento a… is informal; le presento a… is usted. Use ser for who you are and where you are from (soy de…), and estar for how or where you are (estoy bien). ¿De dónde es usted? is the safer origin question with someone you have just met. Con permiso as you come in.

1. You want to introduce a friend to an older person you have just met. Do you say te presento or le presento, and why?

2. Write a line saying you are from the United States and you live in Texas.

3. Why is Soy Elena built with ser, not estar?

4. How do you ask an older person where they are from?

5. What does mis hijos mean, and what does mi hija mean?

6. Someone says Pase usted, siéntese. What do you say as you go in?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
