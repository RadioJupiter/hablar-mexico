Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the unit's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Greet first. Use usted when you are unsure, and tú with friends. ¿Qué onda? is informal. ¿Mande? is the polite way to ask someone to repeat. Plural you is ustedes, never vosotros. H is silent: hola is OH-lah.

1. It is 10 a.m. and you walk into a small shop. What greeting fits?

2. You are speaking to an older person you have just met. Do you ask ¿Cómo estás? or ¿Cómo está?, and why?

3. A friend greets you with ¿Qué onda? Write a natural reply.

4. You did not catch someone's name. What do you say, and why is ¿Qué? a weaker choice here?

5. How do you say “pleased to meet you,” and what is one short reply?

6. When you are talking to two people in Mexico, which plural you do you use?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
