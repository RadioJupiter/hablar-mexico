Likes, plans, and the weather

Unit 8 · Likes, plans, and the weather · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the unit's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Me gusta + one thing or an activity, me gustan + several things. Ask back with ¿y a ti? and answer a mí también or a mí no. Plans: voy a + verb. Weather: hace calor / frío / sol, está nublado, está lloviendo, va a llover.

1. Why is it me gusta el café but me gustan los tacos?

2. Ask a friend (tú) whether they like spicy food. Then ask the same of someone you address as usted.

3. Someone says Me gusta caminar. You like it too. What do you say?

4. Say that you are going to rest this weekend.

5. Say “It's cold,” “It's cloudy,” and “It's going to rain.”

6. What does Ojalá mean in reply to Va a llover?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
