Remember

Suggest a time with a las… and check it with ¿Te queda bien? Informal agreement is ¿Sale? / Sale. At the door: pásale (or pase usted), and you say con permiso. At the table: provecho, está rico, and gracias por la comida. Ahorita can mean right now, in a moment, or a looser soon; read it from context.

1. How do you say “at 7:00,” “at 7:30,” and “around 7:00”?

2. What does ¿Sale? ask, and is it formal or informal?

3. You are at the door of a friend's house and they say ¡Pásale! What do you say as you go in?

4. Someone is already eating as you pass the table. What courtesy can you say?

5. Give two possible meanings of ahorita, and one pair of phrases to use when you need to be more precise.

6. How do you thank someone for the meal as you leave the table?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
