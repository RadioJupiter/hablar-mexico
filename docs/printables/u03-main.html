Remember

Ask a stall price with ¿A cómo está el kilo? or ¿Cuánto cuesta? Totals use son + the number + pesos. Medio kilo is half a kilo. Do not mix up quince (15) and cincuenta (50), or sesenta (60) and setenta (70). Amounts in this unit are invented.

1. Write two different ways to ask the price of a kilo of tomatoes (jitomate).

2. How do you ask for half a kilo of limes?

3. What is the difference between está a treinta pesos el kilo and son treinta pesos?

4. Write the Spanish numbers for 15, 50, 60, and 70. What is easy to mix up?

5. You need change for a 500-peso bill. What do you ask?

6. In the model, the oranges are 22 pesos a kilo and you buy two kilos; the limes are 30 a kilo and you buy half a kilo. What is the total, in Spanish?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
