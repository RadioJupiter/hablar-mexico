At a taquería or café

Unit 2 · At a taquería or café · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the unit's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Greet, then order with ¿Me da…? or ¿Me pone…? and por favor. Con todo is “with everything”; sin cebolla leaves the onion off. Para aquí is for here; para llevar is to go. Ask for the bill with La cuenta, por favor. The prices in this unit are invented.

1. Write a polite line ordering two suadero tacos.

2. The cook asks ¿Con todo? You want everything on one taco and no onion on the other. What do you say?

3. What is the difference between para aquí and para llevar?

4. How do you ask for the bill?

5. How do you tell the cook the food is very good?

6. Why does this unit treat the forty pesos in the model as an invented number?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
